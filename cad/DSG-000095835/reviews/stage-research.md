## Modeling interpretation

- Signed linear limits of ±50 mm for a 100 mm Parker stroke assume the imported pose is mid-travel. The CAD carriage position must determine any different offset; the catalog does not identify this instrument's zero.
- Stage axes must follow the actual mounting transforms and bearing geometry in this STEP assembly. Vendor labels such as “X stage” describe the local stage direction, not the instrument's world axis.
- Swivel motion requires a remote center. Rotating SA04B-RM01 about its body center would give incorrect analyzer motion.
- Published maximum speeds for the standard Kohzu stages are reference values. In particular, the XA07A-L201-BM motor variant is not confirmed by the five-phase motor table in the legacy catalog.
- The diffractometer is explicitly represented by `#1755421` (`mo39144450`) and references `#1755422` through `#1755425` (`DiffractometerBase`, `Diffractometer`, `2ThetaArm`, `2ThetaStage`). Whether the separately named Huber 410 belongs to the retained analyzer chain must be decided from assembly hierarchy, not the word “goniometer.”
- No XCS communication/PV mapping was researched or configured; the intended first version is local simulation.
